Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Service — for example, to display your spending history, generate category breakdowns, and let you sign in to your account. We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is only accessible to you through your authenticated account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) to operate the Service. These providers only process data as necessary to support Spendly's functionality and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through your account settings or the support channel listed on our site.
